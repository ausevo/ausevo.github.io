## Overview

This half-day workshop will provide an introduction to phylogenomics. It comprises a series of lectures and practical exercises covering the interpretation of phylogenetic trees, data types, evolutionary models, maximum likelihood, gene trees vs species trees, and phylogenomic analysis.

The workshop will include short practical exercises based on the software MEGA, IQ-TREE, and ASTER. Prior knowledge of basic phylogenetics, or at least familiarity with molecular data and interpreting phylogenetic trees, is very helpful.

## Instructors

The workshop will be run by **Prof Simon Ho** and **Dr Manuela Cascini** from the Molecular Ecology, Evolution, and Phylogenomics Laboratory, School of Life and Environmental Sciences, University of Sydney.

## Who should attend

It helps to know some basic phylogenetics, or at least to be familiar with molecular data and reading phylogenetic trees.

## Resources and computing

Workshop materials, including lecture slides and files for the practical exercises, will be available on GitHub prior to the start of the workshop.

The practical exercises involve basic analyses of prepared data sets. Please **bring your own laptop** with the following software installed: **MEGA 12**, **IQ-TREE 3**, **ASTER**, and **FigTree**.
