## Conference Program

**Evolution in the Gong (Joint AES x SASB Conference) 2026** will be held at the **University of Wollongong**, Wollongong, NSW, **2–4 December 2026** hosted by Damien Esquerre (AES) and Nik Johnson (SASB).
